# Elset: Satellite Tracker — Support

Elset puts every active satellite on a live 3D globe and tells you when to look up. It's free on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## Contact

Email: shailesh001@mac.com. Please include your device, its iOS/iPadOS or macOS version, and the Elset version (Settings ▸ About).

Privacy policy: [Elset privacy policy](privacy/)

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?
No. Elset has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

### Why does Elset ask for my location?
To work out which satellites pass over you, and when. Your location is used on your device only, and is never sent to the developer or to any satellite-data service. You can also skip the permission and set a location by searching for a place, typing coordinates or, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, tapping the globe.

### I said no to location. How do I change it?
Elset won't ask again once you've declined. Turn it on in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services ▸ Elset, or set a location by hand in the Observer panel.

### Why didn't a pass alert arrive?
Check that notifications are allowed for Elset in Settings. Alerts are scheduled up to 48 hours ahead, for the soonest 60 passes across all satellites with alerts on, and are topped up each time Elset runs. If "Visible passes only" or "Bright passes only" is on, other passes won't trigger an alert.

### The app says "Offline · showing cached data". Is something wrong?
No. Elset keeps tracking from the last data it downloaded, and positions are still worked out on your device. It refreshes automatically when the connection returns. If a data service is busy, Elset waits and retries, and shows cached data meanwhile.

### How accurate are the positions and passes?
Elset uses the standard SGP4/SDP4 model with the latest published element sets, which are refreshed every three hours. Accuracy is best within a day or two of each element set's date; the detail card shows whether it's Fresh, Aging or Stale. Rise and set times are geometric, without atmospheric refraction, so a satellite near the horizon may appear slightly earlier than shown.

### How do I sync between my devices?
Turn on Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ Sync with iCloud on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account. It syncs your favourites, chosen categories, custom satellites, saved sites and pass-alert settings, through your own private iCloud. Changes usually appear on your other devices within a few seconds. On iPhone and iPad they can arrive in the background too, though if iOS has closed Elset completely, they arrive the next time you open it.

### Can I add a satellite that isn't listed?
Yes. Open the ••• menu ▸ Custom Satellites to search CelesTrak by NORAD number or name, or import element sets (TLE, or CCSDS OMM as JSON, CSV, XML or KVN). Custom holds up to 50 satellites.

### Are the close approaches a collision warning?
No. They come from CelesTrak SOCRATES and are shown for information only, not for operational use.

### Where is the menu bar readout on the Mac?
View ▸ Show in Menu Bar. Closing the globe window leaves Elset running in the menu bar; quit from there.
